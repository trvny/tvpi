# TRAVNY

> Public hub for TVP IPTV, local weather, enhanced feeds, browser workbenches and small technical publications.

Canonical site: https://trfny.com/. `travny.pages.dev` is a noindex emergency fallback; `tvpi.pages.dev` redirects to the canonical host.

## Services

- [TVPI](https://trfny.com/tv/): browser page for stable TVP IPTV entry points and playlists.
- [Weather](https://weather.trfny.com/): multi-source local weather for Kościelec in Chrzanów.
- [Feedseek](https://trvny.github.io/feedseek/): enhanced Atom/RSS directory, JSON Feed sidecars, OPML and Reader.
- [Streambench](https://streambench.trfny.com/): IPTV, radio, HLS, M3U/M3U8 and XMLTV workshop.
- [Code Bench](https://codebench.trfny.com/): local-first QR and barcode studio.
- [Doc Bench](https://docbench.travny.workers.dev/): local-first document and PDF studio.
- [Teksty](https://trfny.com/teksty/): experiments, comparisons and short technical publications.
